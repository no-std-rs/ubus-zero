Use a GUITION ESP32-2424S012-V1.0 with 4 MB flash and a USB data cable. The installer supports this board’s GC9A01 display and CST816 touch wiring.

Configure the read-only dashboard account on your router first. Installation replaces the device firmware; the installer lets you choose whether to erase its flash.
